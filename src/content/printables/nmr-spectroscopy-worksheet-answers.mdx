## A. Environments

<Q n={1}>Four kinds of information.</Q>
<Answer>(1) **Number of signals**: number of H environments. (2) **Chemical shift δ**: the type of environment. (3) **Integration**: relative number of H in each environment. (4) **Splitting**: number of H on neighbouring carbons.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Signals.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **1** (all 6 H equivalent) (b) **2**, ratio **6 : 2** (= 3 : 1) (c) **2**, ratio **9 : 1** (d) **1** (all 6 H equivalent) (e) **3**: CH₃ of ethyl, CH₂, and O–CH₃, ratio **3 : 2 : 3**</Answer>

## C. Splitting and predicting spectra

<Q n={4}>Splitting.</Q>
<Answer>(a) $\ce{CH2}$ (next to 3 H): **quartet**, about 3.6 ppm; $\ce{CH3}$ (next to 2 H): **triplet**, about 1.5 ppm. (b) $\ce{CH}$ (next to 6 equivalent H): **septet** (7 peaks); each $\ce{CH3}$ (next to 1 H): **doublet**, area 6.</Answer>

## D. Solving structures

<Q n={7}>C₃H₈O.</Q>
<Answer>**Propan-2-ol**, $\ce{(CH3)2CHOH}$. Doublet (6H) at 1.2 ppm: two equivalent $\ce{CH3}$ groups next to one H. Septet (1H) at 4.0 ppm: the $\ce{CH}$ on the carbon bonded to O, next to 6 H. Singlet (1H) at 2.2 ppm: the $\ce{OH}$.</Answer>

## E. Challenge

<Q n={8}>C₄H₈O.</Q>
<Answer>**Butanone**, $\ce{CH3COCH2CH3}$. IR 1715 cm⁻¹: **C=O** (no O–H). MS m/z 72: molar mass of C₄H₈O. NMR: singlet (3H) at 2.1 ppm: $\ce{CH3}$ next to C=O with no neighbouring H; quartet (2H) at 2.4 ppm: $\ce{CH2}$ next to C=O and next to a $\ce{CH3}$; triplet (3H) at 1.1 ppm: the $\ce{CH3}$ of the ethyl group, next to $\ce{CH2}$.</Answer>
