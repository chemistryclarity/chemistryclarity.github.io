## A. The idea

<Q n={1}>Definition.</Q>
<Answer>Density is the **mass per unit volume** of a substance: $\rho = \dfrac{m}{V}$. Common units: **g/cm³** (solids), **g/mL** (liquids) and the SI unit **kg/m³**.</Answer>

## B. Calculating density

<Q n={2}>Densities.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\rho = \dfrac{96.0\ \text{g}}{12.0\ \text{cm}^3} = \textbf{8.00 g/cm}^{\textbf{3}}$

(b) Convert first: 2.50 kg = 2500 g and 0.500 L = 500. mL.

$\rho = \dfrac{2500\ \text{g}}{500.\ \text{mL}} = \textbf{5.00 g/mL}$

</Answer>

<Q n={3}>Metal cube.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $V = (3.00\ \text{cm})^3 = 27.0\ \text{cm}^3$, so $\rho = \dfrac{241\ \text{g}}{27.0\ \text{cm}^3} = \textbf{8.93 g/cm}^{\textbf{3}}$

(b) Closest value: **copper** (8.96 g/cm³).

(c)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{error} = \frac{8.926\ \text{g/cm}^3 - 8.96\ \text{g/cm}^3}{8.96\ \text{g/cm}^3} \times 100\% \\[4pt]
&= \boldsymbol{-0.4\%}
\end{aligned}
$$

</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Displacement.</Q>
<Answer>

$V = 45.2\ \text{mL} - 30.0\ \text{mL} = \textbf{15.2 mL} = 15.2\ \text{cm}^3$

$\rho = \dfrac{68.4\ \text{g}}{15.2\ \text{cm}^3} = \textbf{4.50 g/cm}^{\textbf{3}}$, closest to **titanium** (4.51 g/cm³).

</Answer>

## C. Using density

<Q n={5}>Layers.</Q>
<Answer>(a) From the top: **oil** (0.92 g/cm³), **water** (0.998 g/cm³), **honey** (1.42 g/cm³) at the bottom. (b) The bead (1.10 g/cm³) is denser than water but less dense than honey, so it sinks through the oil and water and **rests on top of the honey layer**.</Answer>

<Q n={6}>Mass and volume.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $m = \rho V = 0.789\ \dfrac{\text{g}}{\cancel{\text{mL}}} \times 200.\ \cancel{\text{mL}} = 157.8\ \text{g} = \textbf{158 g}$

(b) 1.00 kg = 1000 g, so $V = \dfrac{m}{\rho} = \dfrac{1000\ \text{g}}{11.3\ \text{g/cm}^3} = \textbf{88.5 cm}^{\textbf{3}}$

</Answer>

## D. Replicate measurements

<Q n={7}>Five replicates.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) Mean:

$$
\bar{\rho} = \frac{(2.68 + 2.71 + 2.69 + 2.72 + 2.70)\ \text{g/cm}^3}{5} = \frac{13.50\ \text{g/cm}^3}{5} = 2.70\ \text{g/cm}^3
$$

Deviations: −0.02, +0.01, −0.01, +0.02 and 0.00 g/cm³; the sum of their squares is $1.0 \times 10^{-3}\ (\text{g/cm}^3)^2$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&s = \sqrt{\frac{1.0 \times 10^{-3}\ (\text{g/cm}^3)^2}{5 - 1}} \\[4pt]
&= 0.016\ \text{g/cm}^3
\end{aligned}
$$

(b) **2.70 ± 0.02 g/cm³ (n = 5)**

(c) The mean equals the accepted value for aluminium (2.70 g/cm³), so the result is **accurate**; the standard deviation is small (under 1 % of the mean), so it is **precise**.

</Answer>

## E. Challenge

<Q n={8}>The crown.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) 1.50 kg = 1500 g and 93.0 mL = 93.0 cm³:

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\rho = \frac{1500\ \text{g}}{93.0\ \text{cm}^3} = \textbf{16.1 g/cm}^{\textbf{3}} \\[4pt]
&16.1\ \frac{\cancel{\text{g}}}{\cancel{\text{cm}^3}} \times \frac{1\ \text{kg}}{1000\ \cancel{\text{g}}} \times \frac{10^{6}\ \cancel{\text{cm}^3}}{1\ \text{m}^3} \\[4pt]
&= \boldsymbol{1.61 \times 10^{4}\ \textbf{kg/m}^{3}}
\end{aligned}
$$

(b) **No.** Pure gold is 19.3 g/cm³. The crown's lower density shows it contains a less dense metal, such as silver or copper, mixed with the gold.

</Answer>
