## A. Units and prefixes

<Q n={1}>SI base units.</Q>
<Answer>(a) kilogram, **kg** (b) metre, **m** (c) second, **s** (d) kelvin, **K** (e) mole, **mol**</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Powers of ten.</Q>
<Answer>(a) 1 μg = $10^{-6}$ g (b) 1 GHz = $10^{9}$ Hz (c) 1 nm = $10^{-9}$ m</Answer>

## B. One-step conversions

<Q n={3}>Convert.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $2.5\ \cancel{\text{km}} \times \dfrac{1000\ \text{m}}{1\ \cancel{\text{km}}} = \boldsymbol{2.5 \times 10^{3}\ \textbf{m}}$ (2500 m)

(b) $350\ \cancel{\text{mL}} \times \dfrac{1\ \text{L}}{1000\ \cancel{\text{mL}}} = \textbf{0.350 L}$

(c) $45\ \cancel{\text{mg}} \times \dfrac{1\ \text{g}}{1000\ \cancel{\text{mg}}} = \textbf{0.045 g}$

(d) $0.0045\ \cancel{\text{kg}} \times \dfrac{1000\ \cancel{\text{g}}}{1\ \cancel{\text{kg}}} \times \dfrac{1000\ \text{mg}}{1\ \cancel{\text{g}}} = \boldsymbol{4.5 \times 10^{3}\ \textbf{mg}}$

(e) $500.\ \cancel{\text{nm}} \times \dfrac{10^{-9}\ \text{m}}{1\ \cancel{\text{nm}}} = \boldsymbol{5.00 \times 10^{-7}\ \textbf{m}}$

</Answer>

## C. Areas and volumes

<Q n={4}>Convert.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $5.0\ \cancel{\text{m}^2} \times \dfrac{10^{4}\ \text{cm}^2}{1\ \cancel{\text{m}^2}} = \boldsymbol{5.0 \times 10^{4}\ \textbf{cm}^{2}}$ (because 1 m² = (100 cm)²)

(b) $250\ \cancel{\text{cm}^3} \times \dfrac{1\ \text{m}^3}{10^{6}\ \cancel{\text{cm}^3}} = \boldsymbol{2.50 \times 10^{-4}\ \textbf{m}^{3}}$

(c) $1.0\ \cancel{\text{m}^3} \times \dfrac{1000\ \text{L}}{1\ \cancel{\text{m}^3}} = \boldsymbol{1.0 \times 10^{3}\ \textbf{L}}$ (1 m³ = 1000 dm³ = 1000 L)

(d) $0.250\ \cancel{\text{L}} \times \dfrac{1000\ \text{cm}^3}{1\ \cancel{\text{L}}} = \boldsymbol{\textbf{250. cm}^{3}}$

</Answer>

## D. Multi-step conversions

<Q n={5}>1.5 h.</Q>
<Answer>$1.5\ \cancel{\text{h}} \times \dfrac{3600\ \text{s}}{1\ \cancel{\text{h}}} = \boldsymbol{5.4 \times 10^{3}\ \textbf{s}}$</Answer>

<Q n={6}>36 km/h.</Q>
<Answer>

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\frac{36\ \cancel{\text{km}}}{1\ \cancel{\text{h}}} \times \frac{1000\ \text{m}}{1\ \cancel{\text{km}}} \times \frac{1\ \cancel{\text{h}}}{3600\ \text{s}} \\[4pt]
&= \textbf{10 m/s}
\end{aligned}
$$

</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Iron.</Q>
<Answer>

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\frac{7.86\ \cancel{\text{g}}}{1\ \cancel{\text{cm}^3}} \times \frac{1\ \text{kg}}{1000\ \cancel{\text{g}}} \times \frac{10^{6}\ \cancel{\text{cm}^3}}{1\ \text{m}^3} \\[4pt]
&= \boldsymbol{7.86 \times 10^{3}\ \textbf{kg/m}^{3}}
\end{aligned}
$$

</Answer>

<Q n={8}>Mercury.</Q>
<Answer>$15.0\ \cancel{\text{mL}} \times \dfrac{13.6\ \text{g}}{1\ \cancel{\text{mL}}} = \textbf{204 g}$</Answer>

## E. Challenge

<Q n={9}>Swimming pool.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $V = 25.0\ \text{m} \times 10.0\ \text{m} \times 2.00\ \text{m} = \boldsymbol{\textbf{500. m}^{3}}$

$500.\ \cancel{\text{m}^3} \times \dfrac{1000\ \text{L}}{1\ \cancel{\text{m}^3}} = \boldsymbol{5.00 \times 10^{5}\ \textbf{L}}$

(b)

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= 5.00 \times 10^{5}\ \cancel{\text{L}} \times \frac{1000\ \cancel{\text{mL}}}{1\ \cancel{\text{L}}} \\[4pt]
&\quad \times \frac{1.00\ \cancel{\text{g}}}{1\ \cancel{\text{mL}}} \times \frac{1\ \text{kg}}{1000\ \cancel{\text{g}}} \\[4pt]
&= \boldsymbol{5.00 \times 10^{5}\ \textbf{kg}}
\end{aligned}
$$

</Answer>
