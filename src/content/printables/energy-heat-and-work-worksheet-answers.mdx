## A. Ideas

<Q n={1}>Definitions and systems.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) **Energy**: the capacity to do work or transfer heat. **Heat**: energy transferred because of a temperature difference. **Work**: energy transferred by a force acting through a distance (e.g. a gas pushing a piston).

(b) Boiling pan: **open** (steam and heat escape). Sealed balloon: **closed** (heat can pass, gas cannot). Insulated sealed flask: **isolated** (ideally, neither passes).

</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Bath and cup.</Q>
<Answer>The **cup** has the higher temperature (100 °C). The **bath** contains more thermal energy, because it has far more water: temperature reflects the **average** energy per particle, while the total thermal energy also depends on how many particles there are.</Answer>

## D. Work and the first law

<Q n={6}>Compression.</Q>
<Answer>

$\Delta V = 4.0\ \text{L} - 10.0\ \text{L} = -6.0\ \text{L}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&w = -P_\text{ext}\,\Delta V = -2.00\ \text{atm} \times (-6.0\ \text{L}) \\[4pt]
&= +12\ \cancel{\text{L atm}} \times \frac{101.325\ \text{J}}{1\ \cancel{\text{L atm}}} \\[4pt]
&= \boldsymbol{+1.2 \times 10^{3}\ \textbf{J}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Positive: the surroundings do work **on** the gas, so the system gains energy.

</Answer>

<Q n={7}>ΔU.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\Delta U = (-300.\ \text{J}) + (+150.\ \text{J}) = \boldsymbol{-150.\ \textbf{J}}$

(b) $\Delta U = (+500.\ \text{J}) + (-200.\ \text{J}) = \textbf{+300. J}$

(c) Rigid container: $\Delta V = 0$, so $w = 0$ and $\Delta U = q = \textbf{+75 J}$

</Answer>

## E. Challenge

<Q n={8}>Piston.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\Delta V = 6.00\ \text{L} - 1.00\ \text{L} = 5.00\ \text{L}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&w = -1.00\ \text{atm} \times 5.00\ \text{L} = -5.00\ \cancel{\text{L atm}} \\[4pt]
&\quad \times \frac{101.325\ \text{J}}{1\ \cancel{\text{L atm}}} = \boldsymbol{-507\ \textbf{J}}
\end{aligned}
$$

(b) $q = +2.50\ \text{kJ} = +2500\ \text{J}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta U = q + w = (+2500\ \text{J}) + (-507\ \text{J}) \\[4pt]
&= +1993\ \text{J} = \textbf{+1.99 kJ}
\end{aligned}
$$

(c) ΔU is positive, so the internal energy **increased**: the gas gained more energy as heat than it lost as work.

</Answer>
