## A. The particles

<Q n={1}>Charge, mass and location.</Q>
<Answer>

Proton: charge +1, mass about 1 u, in the nucleus.

Neutron: charge 0, mass about 1 u, in the nucleus.

Electron: charge −1, mass about 1/1836 of a proton, around the nucleus.

</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Atomic number and mass number.</Q>
<Answer>Atomic number = number of **protons** (identifies the element). Mass number = number of **protons + neutrons**.</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Write the symbols.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) 26 protons = iron; A = 26 + 30 = 56; 24 electrons gives 2+: **$\ce{^{56}_{26}Fe^2+}$**

(b) 8 protons = oxygen; A = 8 + 10 = 18; 10 electrons gives 2−: **$\ce{^{18}_{8}O^2-}$**

</Answer>

## C. Isotopes

<Q n={5}>Neutrons in hydrogen isotopes.</Q>
<Answer>Hydrogen-1: **0**; hydrogen-2: **1**; hydrogen-3: **2** (each has 1 proton).</Answer>

<Q n={6}>Why isotopes behave the same chemically.</Q>
<Answer>They have the same number of protons and therefore, as neutral atoms, the same number of **electrons**. Chemical behaviour depends on the electrons, not the neutrons.</Answer>

## D. Challenge

<Q n={7}>Why 35.45?</Q>
<Answer>35.45 is the **weighted average** mass of chlorine's naturally occurring isotopes (mainly chlorine-35 and chlorine-37). Mass numbers are whole-number counts for individual isotopes.</Answer>
