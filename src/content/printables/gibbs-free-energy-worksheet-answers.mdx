## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>Concepts.</Q>
<Answer>(a) A measure of how **spread out** energy and matter are (the number of ways particles and energy can be arranged). (b) In a spontaneous process, the **total entropy of the universe increases**. (c) ΔG = ΔH − TΔS: melting has a positive ΔS, and at 25 °C the **TΔS term is larger than ΔH**, so ΔG is negative even though ΔH is positive.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Signs of ΔS.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **+** (liquid → gas) (b) **−** (3 mol gas → 2 mol) (c) **+** (ordered solid → mobile ions) (d) **+** (a gas is formed) (e) **close to zero** (2 mol gas on each side)</Answer>

<Q n={3}>Four cases.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **All temperatures** (ΔH −, ΔS +) (b) **Never** (ΔH +, ΔS −) (c) **Only at low T**, below $T = \dfrac{60\ \text{kJ}}{0.150\ \text{kJ/K}} = 400\ \text{K}$ (d) **Only at high T**, above $T = \dfrac{90\ \text{kJ}}{0.200\ \text{kJ/K}} = 450\ \text{K}$</Answer>

## B. Calculations

<Q n={4}>ΔS° of methane combustion.</Q>
<Answer>

$\Delta S° = [213.8 + 2(70.0)] - [186.3 + 2(205.2)] = 353.8 - 596.7 =$ **−242.9 J/K**

Negative because **3 mol of gas** become **1 mol of gas** (the water is liquid).

</Answer>

<Q n={5}>ΔH° and ΔG°.</Q>
<Answer>

$\Delta H° = [-393.5 + 2(-285.8)] - [-74.6 + 2(0)] =$ **−890.5 kJ**

$\Delta G° = -890.5\ \text{kJ} - (298.15\ \text{K})(-0.2429\ \text{kJ/K}) = -890.5\ \text{kJ} + 72.42\ \text{kJ} =$ **−818.1 kJ** (spontaneous)

</Answer>

<Q n={6}>Melting ice.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $T = \dfrac{6.01\ \text{kJ/mol}}{0.0220\ \text{kJ mol}^{-1}\,\text{K}^{-1}} =$ **273 K** (0 °C, the melting point)

(b) At 263.15 K: $\Delta G = 6.01\ \text{kJ/mol} - (263.15\ \text{K})(0.0220\ \text{kJ mol}^{-1}\,\text{K}^{-1}) =$ **+0.221 kJ/mol**: ice does **not** melt.

At 283.15 K: $\Delta G = 6.01\ \text{kJ/mol} - (283.15\ \text{K})(0.0220\ \text{kJ mol}^{-1}\,\text{K}^{-1}) =$ **−0.219 kJ/mol**: ice **melts** spontaneously.

</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Limestone at 1200 K.</Q>
<Answer>

$\Delta G° = 179.2\ \text{kJ} - (1200\ \text{K})(0.1602\ \text{kJ/K}) = 179.2\ \text{kJ} - 192.2\ \text{kJ} =$ **−13.0 kJ**: spontaneous (1200 K is above the switching temperature, 1119 K).

</Answer>

<Q n={8}>K from ΔG°.</Q>
<Answer>

$\ln K = -\dfrac{\Delta G°}{RT} = -\dfrac{1.00 \times 10^{4}\ \text{J/mol}}{(8.314\ \text{J mol}^{-1}\,\text{K}^{-1})(298.15\ \text{K})} = -4.034$

$K = e^{-4.034} =$ **0.0177**: K is less than 1, so **reactants** are favoured.

</Answer>

## C. Challenge

<Q n={9}>Daniell cell.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\Delta G° = -nFE° = -(2\ \text{mol})(96\,485\ \text{C/mol})(1.10\ \text{V}) = -2.123 \times 10^{5}\ \text{J} =$ **−212 kJ** (C × V = J)

(b) $\ln K = -\dfrac{\Delta G°}{RT} = \dfrac{2.123 \times 10^{5}\ \text{J/mol}}{(8.314\ \text{J mol}^{-1}\,\text{K}^{-1})(298.15\ \text{K})} = 85.63$, so $K = e^{85.63} \approx$ **1.5 × 10³⁷**

The reaction goes essentially **to completion**: at equilibrium almost no $\ce{Cu^2+}$ would remain.

</Answer>
