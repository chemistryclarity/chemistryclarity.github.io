## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>What percent composition tells you.</Q>
<Answer>The percentage of the compound's **mass** that comes from each element.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Why hydrogen is only about 25% of methane's mass.</Q>
<Answer>Percent composition is by mass, not number of atoms. Hydrogen atoms (1.008 u) are much lighter than carbon (12.01 u): % H = 4(1.008) ÷ 16.04 × 100% = **25.13%**.</Answer>

## C. Percent composition

<Q n={4}>Percent composition of NaOH (formula mass 40.00 u).</Q>
<Answer>% Na = 22.99 ÷ 40.00 × 100 = **57.48%**; % O = 16.00 ÷ 40.00 × 100 = **40.00%**; % H = 1.008 ÷ 40.00 × 100 = **2.52%** (total 100.00%)</Answer>

<Q n={5}>% N in NH₄NO₃.</Q>
<Answer>Formula mass = 2(14.01) + 4(1.008) + 3(16.00) = 80.05 u. % N = 28.02 ÷ 80.05 × 100 = **35.00%**</Answer>

<Q n={6}>Percent composition of ethanol (formula mass 46.07 u).</Q>
<Answer>% C = 24.02 ÷ 46.07 × 100 = **52.14%**; % H = 6.048 ÷ 46.07 × 100 = **13.13%**; % O = 16.00 ÷ 46.07 × 100 = **34.73%** (total 100.00%)</Answer>

## D. Mass of an element in a sample

<Q n={7}>Copper in 25.0 g of CuSO₄.</Q>
<Answer>Formula mass = 63.55 + 32.06 + 4(16.00) = 159.61 u. Mass of Cu = $\dfrac{63.55}{159.61} \times 25.0 =$ **9.95 g**</Answer>

<Q n={8}>Oxygen in 50.0 g of CaCO₃.</Q>
<Answer>Formula mass = 100.09 u; O contributes 3(16.00) = 48.00 u. Mass of O = $\dfrac{48.00}{100.09} \times 50.0 =$ **24.0 g**</Answer>

## E. Challenge

<Q n={9}>Which fertiliser has more nitrogen by mass?</Q>
<Answer>$\ce{NH3}$: 14.01 ÷ 17.03 × 100 = **82.25%** N. $\ce{(NH4)2SO4}$: 28.02 ÷ 132.14 × 100 = **21.20%** N. **Ammonia** has the higher mass percent of nitrogen.</Answer>

<Q n={10}>Percentage of water in CuSO₄·5H₂O.</Q>
