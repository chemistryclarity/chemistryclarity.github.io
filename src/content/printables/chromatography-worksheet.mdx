## B. Rf values

<Q n={2} box={5}>On a TLC plate the solvent front is 8.40 cm above the baseline. A mixture gives spots at 2.10 cm, 4.62 cm and 6.72 cm. Standards on the same plate: X, Rf 0.25; Y, Rf 0.55; Z, Rf 0.70. (a) Calculate the three Rf values. (b) Which standards are in the mixture? (c) What can you say about the third spot?</Q>

## D. Gas chromatography

<Q n={7} box={4}>Peak areas for a three-component mixture are 2310, 1540 and 3850. Assuming equal detector response, calculate the percentage of each component.</Q>

## E. Replicates and quantities

<Q n={8} box={5}>An unknown dye is run five times (quintuplicate) beside standards. Rf values: 0.61, 0.63, 0.62, 0.60, 0.64. Standards: dye M, Rf 0.62; dye N, Rf 0.71. (a) Calculate the mean and the standard deviation. (b) Which dye is it? Justify using the standard deviation.</Q>

<Q n={9} box={6}>In a GC analysis, a 0.500 mg/mL standard gives a peak area of 2480. A sample is injected three times, giving areas of 1850, 1870 and 1860. (a) Find the mean area, the standard deviation and the RSD. (b) Assuming area is proportional to concentration, find the concentration in the sample. (c) What mass of the compound is in 20.0 mL of the sample?</Q>
