## A. Mole ratios

<Q n={1}>Mole ratios in 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃.</Q>
<Answer>(a) $\ce{O2}$ : $\ce{Al}$ = **3 mol : 4 mol** (b) $\ce{Al2O3}$ : $\ce{Al}$ = **2 mol : 4 mol = 1 : 2**</Answer>

<Q n={2}>O₂ for 6.0 mol Al.</Q>
<Answer>$6.0\ \text{mol Al} \times \dfrac{3\ \text{mol O}_2}{4\ \text{mol Al}} =$ **4.5 mol** of $\ce{O2}$</Answer>

## B. Mass calculations

<Q n={3}>MgO from 12.15 g Mg.</Q>
<Answer>n(Mg) = 12.15 g ÷ 24.31 g/mol = 0.4998 mol → n(MgO) = 0.4998 mol (2 mol MgO : 2 mol Mg). M(MgO) = 24.31 g/mol + 16.00 g/mol = 40.31 g/mol, so mass = 0.4998 mol × 40.31 g/mol = **20.15 g**</Answer>

<Q n={4}>O₂ from 24.5 g KClO₃.</Q>
<Answer>M(KClO₃) = 122.55 g/mol; n = 24.5 g ÷ 122.55 g/mol = 0.1999 mol. n(O₂) = 0.1999 mol KClO₃ × (3 mol O₂ / 2 mol KClO₃) = 0.2999 mol. Mass = 0.2999 mol × 32.00 g/mol = **9.60 g**</Answer>

<Q n={5}>CO₂ from 8.02 g CH₄.</Q>
<Answer>n(CH₄) = 8.02 g ÷ 16.04 g/mol = 0.500 mol → n(CO₂) = 0.500 mol (1 mol CO₂ : 1 mol CH₄). Mass = 0.500 mol × 44.01 g/mol = **22.0 g**</Answer>

<Q n={6}>H₂O from 11.0 g C₃H₈.</Q>
<Answer>n(C₃H₈) = 11.0 g ÷ 44.09 g/mol = 0.2495 mol → n(H₂O) = 0.2495 mol C₃H₈ × (4 mol H₂O / 1 mol C₃H₈) = 0.998 mol. Mass = 0.998 mol × 18.02 g/mol = **18.0 g**</Answer>

## C. Challenge

<Q n={7}>Balance, then find mass of Fe from 100.0 g Fe₂O₃.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\ce{Fe2O3 + 3CO -> 2Fe + 3CO2}$

(b) n(Fe₂O₃) = 100.0 g ÷ 159.70 g/mol = 0.62617 mol → n(Fe) = 0.62617 mol Fe₂O₃ × (2 mol Fe / 1 mol Fe₂O₃) = 1.2523 mol. Mass = 1.2523 mol × 55.85 g/mol = **69.94 g**

</Answer>

<Q n={8}>The 2 g : 1 g mistake.</Q>
<Answer>Coefficients compare **moles**, not grams. 2 mol of Mg (2 mol × 24.31 g/mol = 48.62 g) reacts with 1 mol of O₂ (32.00 g); the mass ratio is not 2 : 1.</Answer>
