## A. Expressions

<Q n={1}>Ksp expressions.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\ce{BaSO4(s) <=> Ba^2+ + SO4^2-}$; $K_\text{sp} = [\ce{Ba^2+}][\ce{SO4^2-}]$

(b) $\ce{Mg(OH)2(s) <=> Mg^2+ + 2OH-}$; $K_\text{sp} = [\ce{Mg^2+}][\ce{OH-}]^2$

(c) $\ce{Ag2CrO4(s) <=> 2Ag+ + CrO4^2-}$; $K_\text{sp} = [\ce{Ag+}]^2[\ce{CrO4^2-}]$

(d) $\ce{Ca3(PO4)2(s) <=> 3Ca^2+ + 2PO4^3-}$; $K_\text{sp} = [\ce{Ca^2+}]^3[\ce{PO4^3-}]^2$

</Answer>

## B. Molar solubility

<Q n={2}>BaSO₄.</Q>
<Answer>$s = \sqrt{1.1 \times 10^{-10}} = \boldsymbol{1.0 \times 10^{-5}}$ mol/L; $1.05 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{mol/L} \times 233.39\ \text{g/mol} = \boldsymbol{2.4 \times 10^{-3}}$ g/L</Answer>

<Q n={3}>Mg(OH)₂.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $K_\text{sp} = 4s^3$, so $s = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{1.8 \times 10^{-11}}{4}} = \boldsymbol{1.7 \times 10^{-4}}$ mol/L

(b) $[\ce{OH-}] = 2s = 2 \times 1.65 \times 10^{-4} = \boldsymbol{3.3 \times 10^{-4}}$ mol/L

(c) pOH $= -\log(3.30 \times 10^{-4}) = 3.48$; pH $= 14.00 - 3.48 = \textbf{10.52}$

</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Ag₂CrO₄.</Q>
<Answer>$[\ce{Ag+}] = 2s$, $[\ce{CrO4^2-}] = s$: $K_\text{sp} = (2s)^2 s = 4s^3 = 4 \times (6.5 \times 10^{-5})^3 = \boldsymbol{1.1 \times 10^{-12}}$</Answer>

<Q n={5}>Comparing.</Q>
<Answer>AgCl: $s = \sqrt{1.8 \times 10^{-10}} = 1.3 \times 10^{-5}$ mol/L. Ag₂CrO₄: $s = 6.5 \times 10^{-5}$ mol/L, **five times larger**. Their Ksp formulas differ ($s^2$ versus $4s^3$), so Ksp values can only be compared directly for salts with the same ion ratio.</Answer>

## C. Common ion and precipitation

<Q n={6}>CaF₂ in NaF.</Q>
<Answer>$[\ce{F-}] \approx 0.010$ mol/L; $K_\text{sp} = s(0.010)^2$, so $s = \dfrac{3.9 \times 10^{-11}}{1.0 \times 10^{-4}} = \boldsymbol{3.9 \times 10^{-7}}$ mol/L, about **550 times less** than in pure water.</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Mixing.</Q>
<Answer>

Total volume 50.0 mL.

$[\ce{Ba^2+}] = 0.010\ \text{mol/L} \times \dfrac{10.0\ \text{mL}}{50.0\ \text{mL}} = 2.0 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{mol/L}$

$[\ce{SO4^2-}] = 0.0050\ \text{mol/L} \times \dfrac{40.0\ \text{mL}}{50.0\ \text{mL}} = 4.0 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{mol/L}$

$Q = (2.0 \times 10^{-3})(4.0 \times 10^{-3}) = 8.0 \times 10^{-6}$, far greater than $K_\text{sp} = 1.1 \times 10^{-10}$: **BaSO₄ precipitates**.

</Answer>

## D. Challenge

<Q n={8}>Milk of magnesia.</Q>
<Answer>$\ce{Mg(OH)2(s) <=> Mg^2+ + 2OH-}$. Stomach acid supplies $\ce{H+}$, which removes $\ce{OH-}$ by forming water ($\ce{H+ + OH- -> H2O}$). By Le Chatelier's principle, the equilibrium shifts to the **right** to replace the $\ce{OH-}$, so more Mg(OH)₂ dissolves, neutralizing the acid.</Answer>
