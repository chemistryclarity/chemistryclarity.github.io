## A. Hybridization

<Q n={1}>Hybridization.</Q>
<Answer>Hybridization is the mixing of an atom's valence orbitals into an equal number of identical hybrid orbitals. Carbon's 2s and 2p orbitals are different and the p orbitals are at 90°, yet methane has **four identical bonds at 109.5°**. Mixing one s and three p orbitals gives four identical **sp³** hybrids pointing to the corners of a tetrahedron.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Hybridization and angles.</Q>
<Answer>(a) 4 domains: **sp³, 109.5°** (b) 3 domains, no lone pair: **sp², 120°** (c) 2 domains: **sp, 180°** (d) 3 bonds + 1 lone pair: **sp³**, about 107° (ideal 109.5°) (e) 3 domains: **sp², 120°** (f) 2 domains: **sp, 180°**</Answer>

## B. Sigma and pi bonds

<Q n={3}>σ and π.</Q>
<Answer>A **σ bond** forms by **head-on** overlap of orbitals along the line joining the nuclei; it allows rotation. A **π bond** forms by **side-on** overlap of two parallel p orbitals, with electron density above and below the line; it is weaker and prevents rotation.</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Counting.</Q>
<Answer>(a) 6 C–H + 1 C–C: **7 σ, 0 π** (b) 4 C–H + C=C: **5 σ, 1 π** (c) N≡N: **1 σ, 2 π** (d) 6 C–H + C=C + C–C: **8 σ, 1 π** (e) two C=O: **2 σ, 2 π**</Answer>

## C. Carbon in organic molecules

<Q n={5}>Propyne.</Q>
<Answer>C1 (≡CH): 2 domains, **sp**, 180°. C2 (–C≡): 2 domains, **sp**, 180°. C3 (CH₃): 4 domains, **sp³**, 109.5°. So the H–C≡C–C chain is linear.</Answer>

<Q n={6}>Isomers.</Q>
<Answer>The C=C contains a **π bond**, so rotation about it is not possible: the two Cl atoms can be fixed on the **same side** (*cis*) or **opposite sides** (*trans*), giving two different compounds. In 1,2-dichloroethane the C–C is only a **σ bond**, which rotates freely, so all arrangements interconvert and there is only one compound.</Answer>

## D. Lone pairs

<Q n={7}>Water and CO₂.</Q>
<Answer>Oxygen in water has 2 bonds **and 2 lone pairs**: 4 domains, sp³, so the molecule is **bent** (about 104.5°). Carbon in CO₂ has 2 double bonds and **no lone pairs**: 2 domains, sp, so the molecule is **linear** (180°).</Answer>

## E. Challenge

<Q n={8}>Methanamide.</Q>
<Answer>(a) C has 3 domains (H, O, N): **sp²**. O has 3 domains (the C and 2 lone pairs): **sp²**. (b) Bonds: C–H, C=O, C–N, 2 × N–H: **5 σ, 1 π**. (c) About **120°** (trigonal planar around C).</Answer>
