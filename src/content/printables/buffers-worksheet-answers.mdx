## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>What a buffer is.</Q>
<Answer>A solution that resists changes in pH when small amounts of acid or base are added. It contains a **weak acid and its conjugate base** (or a weak base and its conjugate acid).</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Which are buffers?</Q>
<Answer>

(a) **No**: HCl is a strong acid and Cl⁻ is too weak a base to remove added acid.

(b) **Yes**: weak acid + its conjugate base.

(c) **Yes**: weak base + its conjugate acid.

(d) **No**: NaOH is a strong base with no weak acid partner.

</Answer>

<Q n={3}>How the buffer works.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\ce{CH3COO- + H3O+ -> CH3COOH + H2O}$

(b) $\ce{CH3COOH + OH- -> CH3COO- + H2O}$

</Answer>

## B. Buffer pH

<Q n={4}>0.10 M acid, 0.15 M acetate.</Q>
<Answer>pH = 4.74 + log(0.15 M / 0.10 M) = 4.74 + 0.18 = **4.92** (the units of M cancel inside the log)</Answer>

<Q n={5}>0.20 M acid, 0.10 M acetate.</Q>
<Answer>pH = 4.74 + log(0.10 M / 0.20 M) = 4.74 − 0.30 = **4.44**</Answer>

<Q n={6}>0.20 M NH₃, 0.10 M NH₄Cl.</Q>
<Answer>pH = 9.25 + log(0.20 M / 0.10 M) = **9.55**</Answer>

## C. Adding acid

<Q n={7}>Add 0.020 mol HCl to 0.500 L of 0.20 M/0.20 M buffer.</Q>
<Answer>

Starting moles: acid = acetate = 0.20 mol/L × 0.500 L = 0.10 mol.

After reaction: acetate = 0.10 mol − 0.020 mol = 0.08 mol; acetic acid = 0.10 mol + 0.020 mol = 0.12 mol.

pH = 4.74 + log(0.08 mol / 0.12 mol) = 4.74 − 0.18 = **4.56**

</Answer>

## D. Challenge

<Q n={8}>Ratio for pH 5.00.</Q>
<Answer>5.00 = 4.74 + log(ratio), so log(ratio) = 0.26 and ratio = 10^0.26 = **1.8** (about 1.8 times as much acetate as acetic acid).</Answer>
