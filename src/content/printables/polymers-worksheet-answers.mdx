## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>Definitions.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **Monomer:** a small molecule that joins with many others to form a polymer. (b) **Repeat unit:** the part of the chain that repeats, drawn in brackets with n. (c) **Addition polymerization:** monomers with C=C join as the double bonds open; no other product. (d) **Condensation polymerization:** monomers with two functional groups join, releasing a small molecule (usually water) at every link.</Answer>

## B. Repeat units and monomers

<Q n={2}>Repeat units.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **–[CH₂–CH(CH₃)]–** (b) **–[CH₂–CHCl]–** (c) **–[CF₂–CF₂]–** (d) **–[CH₂–CH(C₆H₅)]–**, each with n after the brackets.</Answer>

<Q n={3}>Monomers.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **propenenitrile** (acrylonitrile), $\ce{CH2=CHCN}$ (b) **propene**, $\ce{CH2=CHCH3}$ (c) **1,1-dichloroethene**, $\ce{CH2=CCl2}$</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Classification.</Q>
<Answer>**Addition:** poly(ethene), PVC, PTFE. **Condensation:** nylon-6,6, PET, protein.</Answer>

<Q n={5}>Nylon-6,6.</Q>
<Answer>(a) An **amide** link, –CO–NH– (a C=O bonded to an N–H), formed between –COOH and –NH₂. (b) **Water**, H₂O, one molecule per link.</Answer>

## C. Calculations

<Q n={6}>Degree of polymerization.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $n = \dfrac{3.00 \times 10^{5}\ \text{g/mol}}{100.02\ \text{g/mol}} =$ **3.00 × 10³** repeat units

(b) $M = 3.00 \times 10^{3} \times 42.08\ \text{g/mol} =$ **1.26 × 10⁵ g/mol**

</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Fluorine in PTFE.</Q>
<Answer>

Repeat unit $\ce{C2F4}$ has 4 F: $\dfrac{4 \times 19.00\ \text{g/mol}}{100.02\ \text{g/mol}} \times 100\ \% =$ **76.0 %**

</Answer>

<Q n={8}>Water from nylon.</Q>
<Answer>

$n(\text{repeat units}) = \dfrac{1.00 \times 10^{3}\ \text{g}}{226.32\ \text{g/mol}} = 4.419\ \text{mol}$

$n(\ce{H2O}) = 2 \times 4.419\ \text{mol} = 8.837\ \text{mol}$

$m(\ce{H2O}) = 8.837\ \text{mol} \times 18.02\ \text{g/mol} =$ **159 g**

</Answer>

## D. Challenge

<Q n={9}>Burning poly(ethene).</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\ce{C2H4 + 3O2 -> 2CO2 + 2H2O}$ (per repeat unit)

(b) $n(\text{repeat units}) = \dfrac{1.00 \times 10^{3}\ \text{g}}{28.05\ \text{g/mol}} = 35.65\ \text{mol}$; $n(\ce{CO2}) = 2 \times 35.65\ \text{mol} = 71.30\ \text{mol}$

$m(\ce{CO2}) = 71.30\ \text{mol} \times 44.01\ \text{g/mol} = 3.138 \times 10^{3}\ \text{g} =$ **3.14 kg**

(c) Advantage: **energy is recovered** and the waste volume is greatly reduced. Disadvantages: it releases **CO₂** (a greenhouse gas: over three times the mass of the plastic) and may release **toxic gases** (CO from incomplete combustion, HCl from PVC); the raw material is **lost**, whereas recycling saves crude oil and energy.

</Answer>
