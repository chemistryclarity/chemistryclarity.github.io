## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>Dynamic equilibrium.</Q>
<Answer>Both the forward and reverse reactions continue, at **equal rates**, so the concentrations of reactants and products stay **constant**. They are generally **not equal**.</Answer>

## B. Writing Kc

<Q n={2}>Expressions.</Q>
<Answer>(a) $\dfrac{[\ce{NH3}]^2}{[\ce{N2}][\ce{H2}]^3}$ (b) $\dfrac{[\ce{SO3}]^2}{[\ce{SO2}]^2[\ce{O2}]}$ (c) $[\ce{CO2}]$ (solids left out) (d) $\dfrac{[\ce{PCl3}][\ce{Cl2}]}{[\ce{PCl5}]}$</Answer>

## C. Calculating Kc

<Q n={3}>Kc for SO₃.</Q>
<Answer>

$$
K_\text{c} = \frac{(0.160\ \text{M})^2}{(0.0400\ \text{M})^2(0.0200\ \text{M})} \times 1\ \text{M} = \textbf{800.}
$$

Dividing each concentration by $c° = 1$ M makes the units cancel: $\dfrac{0.0256}{(0.00160)(0.0200)} = 800.$

</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Related reactions.</Q>
<Answer>(a) Reverse: $\dfrac{1}{800.} =$ **1.25 × 10⁻³** (b) Coefficients halved: $\sqrt{800.} =$ **28.3**</Answer>

## D. Q versus K

<Q n={5}>Q for N₂O₄.</Q>
<Answer>$Q = \dfrac{(0.0200\ \text{M})^2}{0.100\ \text{M}} \times \dfrac{1}{1\ \text{M}} = \dfrac{0.000400}{0.100} =$ **0.00400**. $Q < K_\text{c}$ (0.0200), so the reaction goes **forward**: more $\ce{NO2}$ forms.</Answer>

## E. ICE tables

<Q n={6}>H₂ + I₂ starting at 0.200 M each.</Q>
<Answer>

Let $x$ be the decrease in $[\ce{H2}]$, in M. Equilibrium: $[\ce{H2}] = [\ce{I2}] = 0.200\ \text{M} - x$; $[\ce{HI}] = 2x$.

$\sqrt{49.0} = 7.00 = \dfrac{2x}{0.200\ \text{M} - x}$, so $1.400\ \text{M} - 7.00x = 2x$ and $x = \dfrac{1.400\ \text{M}}{9.00} = 0.1556\ \text{M}$

$[\ce{HI}] =$ **0.311 M**; $[\ce{H2}] = [\ce{I2}] = 0.200\ \text{M} - 0.1556\ \text{M} =$ **0.0444 M**

</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Kc for PCl₅.</Q>
<Answer>

The Cl₂ formed (0.100 M) equals the PCl₃ formed and the PCl₅ used up. Equilibrium: $[\ce{PCl5}] = 0.500\ \text{M} - 0.100\ \text{M} = 0.400\ \text{M}$; $[\ce{PCl3}] = [\ce{Cl2}] = 0.100\ \text{M}$.

$K_\text{c} = \dfrac{(0.100\ \text{M})(0.100\ \text{M})}{0.400\ \text{M}} \times \dfrac{1}{1\ \text{M}} =$ **0.0250**

</Answer>

## F. Challenge

<Q n={8}>Large and small K.</Q>
<Answer>$K = 1 \times 10^{10}$: the mixture is almost entirely **products** (the reaction goes essentially to completion). $K = 1 \times 10^{-10}$: almost entirely **reactants** (hardly any reaction).</Answer>

<Q n={9}>PCl₅ starting at 0.300 M.</Q>
<Answer>

Let $x$ (in M) = $[\ce{PCl3}] = [\ce{Cl2}]$ at equilibrium; $[\ce{PCl5}] = 0.300\ \text{M} - x$. Working in units of M (so each concentration divided by 1 M):

$$
0.0250 = \frac{x^2}{0.300 - x} \;\Rightarrow\; x^2 + 0.0250x - 0.00750 = 0
$$

$$
x = \frac{-0.0250 + \sqrt{(0.0250)^2 + 4(0.00750)}}{2} = \frac{-0.0250 + 0.1750}{2} = 0.0750
$$

$[\ce{PCl3}] = [\ce{Cl2}] =$ **0.0750 M**; $[\ce{PCl5}] = 0.300\ \text{M} - 0.0750\ \text{M} =$ **0.225 M**. (The other root is negative, so it is rejected.)

</Answer>
