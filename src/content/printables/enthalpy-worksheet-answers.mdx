## B. q = mcΔT

<Q n={3}>Copper.</Q>
<Answer>$q = (40.0\ \text{g})(0.385\ \text{J/(g·°C)})(75.0\ °\text{C} - 25.0\ °\text{C}) =$ **770. J** (g and °C cancel)</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Specific heat.</Q>
<Answer>$c = \dfrac{q}{m \Delta T} = \dfrac{900.\ \text{J}}{(100.\ \text{g})(20.0\ °\text{C})} =$ **0.450 J/(g·°C)** (close to iron, 0.449 J/(g·°C))</Answer>

<Q n={5}>Final temperature.</Q>
<Answer>

$$
\Delta T = \frac{q}{mc} = \frac{5.00 \times 10^{3}\ \text{J}}{(150.\ \text{g})(4.184\ \text{J/(g·°C)})} = 7.97\ °\text{C}
$$

$T_\text{final} = 22.0\ °\text{C} + 7.97\ °\text{C} =$ **30.0 °C**

</Answer>

## C. Calorimetry

<Q n={6}>Ammonium nitrate.</Q>
<Answer>

$\Delta T = 16.0\ °\text{C} - 22.0\ °\text{C} = -6.0\ °\text{C}$

$q_\text{soln} = (108.0\ \text{g})(4.184\ \text{J/(g·°C)})(-6.0\ °\text{C}) = -2711\ \text{J}$, so $q_\text{reaction} = +2711$ J

$n = \dfrac{8.00\ \text{g}}{80.04\ \text{g/mol}} = 0.09995$ mol

$\Delta H = \dfrac{+2711\ \text{J}}{0.09995\ \text{mol}} = +2.7 \times 10^{4}\ \text{J/mol} =$ **+27 kJ/mol** (endothermic; 2 significant figures from ΔT)

</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Neutralization.</Q>
<Answer>

Mass of solution = 50.0 mL × 1.00 g/mL = 50.0 g; $\Delta T = 33.5\ °\text{C} - 20.0\ °\text{C} = 13.5\ °\text{C}$

$q_\text{soln} = (50.0\ \text{g})(4.184\ \text{J/(g·°C)})(13.5\ °\text{C}) = 2824\ \text{J}$, so $q_\text{reaction} = -2824$ J

$n(\ce{H2O}) = 2.00\ \text{mol/L} \times 0.0250\ \text{L} = 0.0500$ mol

$\Delta H = \dfrac{-2824\ \text{J}}{0.0500\ \text{mol}} = -5.65 \times 10^{4}\ \text{J/mol} =$ **−56.5 kJ/mol**

</Answer>

<Q n={9}>Iron in water.</Q>
<Answer>

Heat lost by iron = heat gained by water:

$(50.0\ \text{g})(0.449\ \text{J/(g·°C)})(100.0\ °\text{C} - T) = (100.\ \text{g})(4.184\ \text{J/(g·°C)})(T - 20.0\ °\text{C})$

$22.45\ \text{J/°C} \times (100.0\ °\text{C} - T) = 418.4\ \text{J/°C} \times (T - 20.0\ °\text{C})$

$2245\ \text{J} + 8368\ \text{J} = (418.4 + 22.45)\ \text{J/°C} \times T$, so $T = \dfrac{10613\ \text{J}}{440.85\ \text{J/°C}} =$ **24.1 °C**

</Answer>
