## C. Calculating Kc

<Q n={3} box={3}>An equilibrium mixture of $\ce{2SO2(g) + O2(g) <=> 2SO3(g)}$ contains 0.0400 M $\ce{SO2}$, 0.0200 M $\ce{O2}$ and 0.160 M $\ce{SO3}$. Calculate $K_\text{c}$.</Q>

## D. Q versus K

<Q n={5} box={3}>For $\ce{N2O4(g) <=> 2NO2(g)}$, $K_\text{c} = 0.0200$. A mixture contains 0.100 M $\ce{N2O4}$ and 0.0200 M $\ce{NO2}$. Calculate Q and predict the direction of change.</Q>

## E. ICE tables

<Q n={6} box={5}>0.200 M $\ce{H2}$ and 0.200 M $\ce{I2}$ are mixed: $\ce{H2(g) + I2(g) <=> 2HI(g)}$, $K_\text{c} = 49.0$. Find the equilibrium concentrations of all three gases.</Q>

<Q n={7} box={4}>0.500 M $\ce{PCl5}$ is placed in a container: $\ce{PCl5(g) <=> PCl3(g) + Cl2(g)}$. At equilibrium, $[\ce{Cl2}] = 0.100$ M. Calculate $K_\text{c}$.</Q>
