## B. Predicting products

<Q n={2}>Will it react?</Q>
<Answer>

(a) Mg is above Zn: $\boldsymbol{\ce{Mg + ZnCl2 -> MgCl2 + Zn}}$

(b) Ag is below Cu: **no reaction**

(c) Zn is above H: $\boldsymbol{\ce{Zn + 2HCl -> ZnCl2 + H2}}$

(d) Cl is more reactive than Br: $\boldsymbol{\ce{Cl2 + 2NaBr -> 2NaCl + Br2}}$

(e) Br is less reactive than Cl: **no reaction**

</Answer>

<Q n={3}>Complete and balance.</Q>

(a) $\ce{2Na + Cl2 -> 2NaCl}$ (b) $\ce{MgCO3 -> MgO + CO2}$ (c) $\ce{H2SO4 + 2NaOH -> Na2SO4 + 2H2O}$

</Answer>

## C. Combustion

<Q n={4}>Combustion.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\ce{CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O}$

(b) $\ce{2C4H10 + 13O2 -> 8CO2 + 10H2O}$

(c) **Carbon monoxide** (CO), a poisonous gas (or soot, C).

</Answer>

## D. Double displacement

<Q n={5}>Why one reacts.</Q>
<Answer>Silver nitrate + sodium chloride gives **silver chloride, which is insoluble**, so a precipitate forms and the reaction happens: $\ce{AgNO3 + NaCl -> AgCl(s) + NaNO3}$. With potassium nitrate + sodium chloride, the possible products (KCl and NaNO₃) are **both soluble**: no precipitate, water or gas, so no reaction.</Answer>

## E. Calculations

<Q n={6}>Magnesium oxide.</Q>
<Answer>

$$
\begin{aligned}
&2.43\ \cancel{\text{g Mg}} \times \frac{1\ \cancel{\text{mol Mg}}}{24.31\ \cancel{\text{g Mg}}} \\[4pt]
&\quad \times \frac{2\ \cancel{\text{mol MgO}}}{2\ \cancel{\text{mol Mg}}} \times \frac{40.31\ \text{g MgO}}{1\ \cancel{\text{mol MgO}}} \\[4pt]
&= \textbf{4.03 g MgO}
\end{aligned}
$$

</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Zinc used.</Q>
<Answer>

$\ce{Zn + CuSO4 -> ZnSO4 + Cu}$: 1 mol Zn gives 1 mol Cu.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&4.86\ \cancel{\text{g Cu}} \times \frac{1\ \cancel{\text{mol Cu}}}{63.55\ \cancel{\text{g Cu}}} \\[4pt]
&\quad \times \frac{1\ \cancel{\text{mol Zn}}}{1\ \cancel{\text{mol Cu}}} \times \frac{65.38\ \text{g Zn}}{1\ \cancel{\text{mol Zn}}} \\[4pt]
&= \textbf{5.00 g Zn}
\end{aligned}
$$

</Answer>

## F. Challenge

<Q n={8}>Order of reactivity.</Q>
<Answer>**X > Z > Y**. A metal can only displace a less reactive metal. X displaces both others, so it is the most reactive; Z displaces Y, so Z is more reactive than Y.</Answer>
