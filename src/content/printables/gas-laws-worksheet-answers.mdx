## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>Particles and pressure.</Q>
<Answer>Pressure comes from gas particles colliding with the container walls. In a smaller volume the same particles hit the walls **more often**, so the pressure is higher.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Why kelvin?</Q>
<Answer>Volume and pressure are proportional to the **absolute** temperature, which starts at absolute zero (0 K). The Celsius scale starts at an arbitrary point, so ratios of Celsius temperatures are meaningless (and 0 °C would mean dividing by zero).</Answer>

## B. Simple gas laws

<Q n={3}>Boyle.</Q>
<Answer>$V_2 = \dfrac{P_1V_1}{P_2} = \dfrac{100 \times 5.0}{250} =$ **2.0 L**</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Charles.</Q>
<Answer>$T_1 = 293.15$ K, $T_2 = 243.15$ K. $V_2 = 250 \times \dfrac{243.15}{293.15} =$ **207 mL**</Answer>

<Q n={5}>Gay-Lussac.</Q>
<Answer>$T_1 = 288.15$ K, $T_2 = 318.15$ K. $P_2 = 220 \times \dfrac{318.15}{288.15} =$ **243 kPa**</Answer>

## C. Combined gas law

<Q n={6}>Combined.</Q>
<Answer>$V_2 = \dfrac{P_1V_1T_2}{P_2T_1} = \dfrac{1.00 \times 4.00 \times 373.15}{2.50 \times 298.15} =$ **2.00 L**</Answer>

## D. Ideal gas law

<Q n={7}>Pressure.</Q>
<Answer>$P = \dfrac{nRT}{V} = \dfrac{0.250 \times 0.08206 \times 300}{5.00} =$ **1.23 atm**</Answer>

<Q n={8}>Carbon dioxide.</Q>
<Answer>(a) $n = \dfrac{PV}{RT} = \dfrac{1.50 \times 2.00}{0.08206 \times 300.15} =$ **0.122 mol** (b) $M(\ce{CO2}) = 44.01$ g/mol; mass = 0.12181 × 44.01 = **5.36 g**</Answer>
