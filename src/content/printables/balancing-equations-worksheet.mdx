## B. Counting atoms

## C. Balancing

<Q n={5} box={4}>Balance: (a) $\ce{Al + O2 -> Al2O3}$ (b) $\ce{KClO3 -> KCl + O2}$ (c) $\ce{Zn + HCl -> ZnCl2 + H2}$</Q>
