## A. Structure of the table

<Q n={1}>Periods and groups.</Q>
<Answer>(a) A **period** is a horizontal row; a **group** is a vertical column of elements with similar properties. (b) Increasing **atomic number** (number of protons).</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Elements.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **lithium**, alkali metal (b) **bromine**, halogen (c) **argon**, noble gas (d) **calcium**, alkaline earth metal (e) **copper**, transition metal</Answer>

## B. Metals, non-metals and metalloids

<Q n={3}>Classify.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **metal** (b) **non-metal** (c) **metalloid** (d) **metal** (e) **non-metal**. Metals are shiny, conduct heat and electricity, and are malleable and ductile (any two).</Answer>

## C. Valence electrons and ions

<Q n={4}>Valence electrons and ions.</Q>
<Answer>(a) 2, $\ce{Ca^2+}$ (b) 3, $\ce{Al^3+}$ (c) 6, $\ce{O^2-}$ (d) 7, $\ce{F-}$ (e) 5, $\ce{N^3-}$</Answer>

<Q n={5}>Formulas.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\ce{Na+}$, $\ce{O^2-}$: $2(+1) + (-2) = 0$, so $\boldsymbol{\ce{Na2O}}$

(b) $\ce{Ca^2+}$, $\ce{Cl-}$: $(+2) + 2(-1) = 0$, so $\boldsymbol{\ce{CaCl2}}$

(c) $\ce{Al^3+}$, $\ce{S^2-}$: $2(+3) + 3(-2) = 0$, so $\boldsymbol{\ce{Al2S3}}$

(d) $\ce{Mg^2+}$, $\ce{N^3-}$: $3(+2) + 2(-3) = 0$, so $\boldsymbol{\ce{Mg3N2}}$

</Answer>

## D. Blocks

<Q n={6}>Blocks.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **s** (b) **d** (c) **p** (d) **f**. The block is the type of subshell (s, p, d or f) that the element's outermost electrons are filling.</Answer>

## F. Challenge

<Q n={8}>Reactivity down a group.</Q>
<Answer>Down **group 1**, the outer electron is in a shell further from the nucleus and more shielded, so it is held less tightly and **lost more easily**: reactivity increases. Down **group 17**, the atoms are larger, so the nucleus attracts an incoming electron **less strongly**: the halogens gain electrons less easily and reactivity decreases.</Answer>
