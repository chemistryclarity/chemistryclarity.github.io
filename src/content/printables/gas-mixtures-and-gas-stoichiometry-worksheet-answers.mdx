## A. Partial pressures

<Q n={1}>Dalton's law.</Q>
<Answer>The total pressure of a mixture of non-reacting gases is the **sum of the partial pressures**. At fixed V and T, each gas's pressure depends only on its number of moles ($P = nRT/V$), so its share of the pressure equals its share of the moles: $P_\text{A} = x_\text{A}P_\text{total}$.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Air.</Q>
<Answer>

N₂: $0.781 \times 1.00\ \text{atm} = \textbf{0.781 atm}$ = **79.1 kPa**

O₂: $0.209 \times 1.00\ \text{atm} = \textbf{0.209 atm}$ = **21.2 kPa**

Ar: $0.0093 \times 1.00\ \text{atm} = \textbf{0.0093 atm}$ = **0.94 kPa**

</Answer>

<Q n={3}>Helium and oxygen.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $n_\text{He} = \dfrac{2.00\ \text{g}}{4.003\ \text{g/mol}} = 0.4996\ \text{mol}$; $n_{\text{O}_2} = \dfrac{8.00\ \text{g}}{32.00\ \text{g/mol}} = 0.2500\ \text{mol}$

(b) $x_\text{He} = \dfrac{0.4996\ \text{mol}}{0.7496\ \text{mol}} = \textbf{0.667}$

(c) $T = 27 + 273.15 = 300.15$ K

$$
\begin{aligned}
&P_\text{He} = \frac{0.4996\ \text{mol} \times 0.08206\ \frac{\text{L atm}}{\text{mol K}} \times 300.15\ \text{K}}{10.0\ \text{L}} \\[4pt]
&= \textbf{1.23 atm}
\end{aligned}
$$

$P_{\text{O}_2}$ (half the moles of He) $= \textbf{0.616 atm}$; $P_\text{total} = 1.23\ \text{atm} + 0.616\ \text{atm} = \textbf{1.85 atm}$

</Answer>

## B. Gases collected over water

<Q n={4}>Oxygen over water.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $P_{\text{O}_2} = 748\ \text{mmHg} - 19.8\ \text{mmHg} = 728.2\ \text{mmHg}$; $\dfrac{728.2\ \text{mmHg}}{760\ \text{mmHg/atm}} = \textbf{0.9582 atm}$

(b) $T = 22 + 273.15 = 295.15$ K

$$
\begin{aligned}
&n = \frac{PV}{RT} = \frac{0.9582\ \text{atm} \times 0.500\ \text{L}}{0.08206\ \frac{\text{L atm}}{\text{mol K}} \times 295.15\ \text{K}} \\[4pt]
&= \boldsymbol{0.0198\ \textbf{mol}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Mass: $0.01978\ \text{mol} \times 32.00\ \text{g/mol} = \textbf{0.633 g}$

</Answer>

## C. Gas stoichiometry

<Q n={5}>Hydrogen from zinc.</Q>
<Answer>

$n(\text{Zn}) = \dfrac{1.31\ \text{g}}{65.38\ \text{g/mol}} = 0.02004\ \text{mol} = n(\text{H}_2)$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&V = \frac{nRT}{P} = \frac{0.02004\ \text{mol} \times 0.08206\ \frac{\text{L atm}}{\text{mol K}} \times 298.15\ \text{K}}{1.00\ \text{atm}} \\[4pt]
&= \textbf{0.490 L}
\end{aligned}
$$

</Answer>

<Q n={6}>Propane.</Q>
<Answer>Volume ratios equal mole ratios. (a) $2.00\ \text{L} \times 5 = \textbf{10.0 L O}_\textbf{2}$ (b) $2.00\ \text{L} \times 3 = \textbf{6.00 L CO}_\textbf{2}$</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Molar volume.</Q>
<Answer>$0.250\ \text{mol} \times 22.4\ \text{L/mol} = \textbf{5.60 L}$. 22.4 L/mol applies only at 0 °C and 1 atm; at 25 °C the gas expands (24.5 L/mol), so use $PV = nRT$ instead.</Answer>

## D. Challenge

<Q n={8}>Airbag.</Q>
<Answer>

Moles of N₂:

$$
\begin{aligned}
&n = \frac{PV}{RT} = \frac{1.10\ \text{atm} \times 60.0\ \text{L}}{0.08206\ \frac{\text{L atm}}{\text{mol K}} \times 298.15\ \text{K}} \\[4pt]
&= 2.698\ \text{mol N}_2
\end{aligned}
$$

Moles and mass of NaN₃ (2 NaN₃ : 3 N₂):

$$
\begin{aligned}
&2.698\ \cancel{\text{mol N}_2} \times \frac{2\ \cancel{\text{mol NaN}_3}}{3\ \cancel{\text{mol N}_2}} \times \frac{65.02\ \text{g}}{1\ \cancel{\text{mol NaN}_3}} \\[4pt]
&= \textbf{117 g NaN}_\textbf{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

</Answer>
