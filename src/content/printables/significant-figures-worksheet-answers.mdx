## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>Ruler.</Q>
<Answer>The millimetre marks make 4.3 cm certain, and you can **estimate one more digit** between the marks (4.37 cm). A fourth digit (4.372 cm) would be a guess beyond what the ruler can show.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Accuracy and precision.</Q>
<Answer>**Accuracy** is how close a result is to the true value; **precision** is how close repeated results are to each other. Yes: a balance that reads 0.20 g too high gives results that agree closely (precise) but are all wrong by the same amount (not accurate).</Answer>

## B. Counting significant figures

<Q n={3}>Count.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **2** (b) **4** (c) **4** (d) **5** (e) **ambiguous**: 2, 3 or 4. Write it in scientific notation to show which, e.g. $1.20 \times 10^{3}$ mL for 3.</Answer>

## C. Scientific notation and rounding

<Q n={4}>Scientific notation.</Q>
<Answer>(a) $3.50 \times 10^{-4}$ m (b) $4.56 \times 10^{4}$ g (c) $2.07 \times 10^{-2}$ L</Answer>

<Q n={5}>Round to 3 significant figures.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **2.46 g** (b) **0.00123 m** (c) **$1.88 \times 10^{4}$ s** (18 800 s would be ambiguous) (d) **10.0 mL** (the first dropped digit is 5, so 9.99 rounds up to 10.0)</Answer>

## D. Calculations

<Q n={6}>Multiply and divide.</Q>
<Answer>(a) 3.25 cm × 2.1 cm = 6.825 cm² → **6.8 cm²** (2 s.f.) (b) 100.0 g ÷ 3.0 mL = 33.33 g/mL → **33 g/mL** (2 s.f.) (c) 8.315 J/(mol·K) × 298 K = 2477.87 J/mol → **$2.48 \times 10^{3}$ J/mol** (3 s.f.; the units of K cancel)</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Add and subtract.</Q>
<Answer>(a) 12.0 g + 0.335 g + 4.5 g = 16.835 g → **16.8 g** (1 d.p.) (b) 6.38 cm − 2.1 cm = 4.28 cm → **4.3 cm** (1 d.p.)</Answer>

<Q n={9}>Mean.</Q>
<Answer>Sum = 2.52 g + 2.48 g + 2.51 g = 7.51 g (two decimal places). Mean = 7.51 g ÷ 3 = 2.5033 g → **2.50 g**. The 3 is a counted, exact number, so it does not limit the precision.</Answer>
