## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>Phases.</Q>
<Answer>(a) The **stationary phase** stays in place (paper, silica, a column coating); the **mobile phase** moves through or over it (a solvent or a gas). (b) Each substance is shared between the two phases according to its **attraction** to each. A substance held more strongly by the stationary phase spends more time there and **moves more slowly**, so the substances travel different distances and separate.</Answer>

## B. Rf values

<Q n={2}>Rf values.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\dfrac{2.10\ \text{cm}}{8.40\ \text{cm}} =$ **0.250**; $\dfrac{4.62\ \text{cm}}{8.40\ \text{cm}} =$ **0.550**; $\dfrac{6.72\ \text{cm}}{8.40\ \text{cm}} =$ **0.800**

(b) **X** (0.25) and **Y** (0.55). (c) The spot at Rf 0.800 matches **none** of the standards (Z is 0.70): it is a different, unidentified substance.

</Answer>

<Q n={3}>Technique.</Q>
<Answer>(a) Pencil (graphite) does not dissolve; **ink would separate** and add spots. (b) So the spots are **not washed into the solvent**. (c) To keep the air **saturated with solvent vapour**, so the solvent does not evaporate from the plate and the front moves evenly. (d) The front **evaporates quickly** and becomes invisible, and its distance is needed for Rf.</Answer>

## C. Polarity

<Q n={4}>Polarity.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **Hexane > propanone > ethanoic acid.** Silica is very polar. Non-polar hexane is held least and moves furthest; propanone is polar (C=O); ethanoic acid is the most polar and can **hydrogen-bond** to the silica, so it moves least. (b) A more polar solvent competes better for the compounds, so **all the Rf values increase**.</Answer>

<Q n={5}>One spot.</Q>
<Answer>**No.** Two substances can have the **same Rf** in one solvent. Run the sample again with a **different solvent** (or stationary phase), or use another method such as GC or a melting point; a pure sample gives one spot under every condition.</Answer>

## D. Gas chromatography

<Q n={6}>Retention times.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **Ethanol** and **butan-1-ol** (the retention times match the standards; no propan-1-ol). (b) Ethanol has the **lowest boiling point** (smallest molecule, weakest attractions), so it spends most time in the gas phase and leaves the column first.</Answer>

## E. Replicates and quantities

<Q n={8}>Quintuplicate Rf.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) Mean $= \dfrac{0.61 + 0.63 + 0.62 + 0.60 + 0.64}{5} =$ **0.62**; $s =$ **0.016** (sample standard deviation, $n - 1 = 4$)

(b) **Dye M.** Its Rf (0.62) equals the mean. Dye N (0.71) differs from the mean by 0.09, more than 5 standard deviations, so it does not match.

</Answer>

<Q n={9}>GC quantification.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) Mean area $= \dfrac{1850 + 1870 + 1860}{3} =$ **1860**; $s =$ **10**; RSD $= \dfrac{10}{1860} \times 100\ \% =$ **0.54 %**

(b) $c = 0.500\ \text{mg/mL} \times \dfrac{1860}{2480} =$ **0.375 mg/mL** (the areas cancel)

(c) $m = 0.375\ \text{mg/mL} \times 20.0\ \text{mL} =$ **7.50 mg**

</Answer>
