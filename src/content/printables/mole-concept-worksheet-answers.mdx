## A. Understanding the mole

<Q n={1}>What is a mole?</Q>
<Answer>A mole is the amount of substance that contains 6.022 × 10²³ particles (atoms, molecules, ions or formula units).</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Same number of atoms?</Q>
<Answer>Yes. Both are one mole, so both contain 6.022 × 10²³ atoms. The masses differ because a magnesium atom is heavier than a carbon atom.</Answer>

<Q n={3}>What is wrong?</Q>
<Answer>Oxygen gas is made of $\ce{O2}$ molecules. One mole contains 6.022 × 10²³ *molecules*, which is 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 1.204 × 10²⁴ oxygen *atoms*.</Answer>

## C. Converting between mass, moles and particles

<Q n={6}>Moles in 25.0 g of $\ce{CH4}$.</Q>
<Answer>$n = \dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{25.0\ \text{g}}{16.04\ \text{g/mol}} =$ **1.56 mol**</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Mass of 0.150 mol of $\ce{CaCO3}$.</Q>
<Answer>$m = n \times M = 0.150\ \text{mol} \times 100.09\ \text{g/mol} =$ **15.0 g**</Answer>

<Q n={8}>Molecules in 0.500 mol of $\ce{NH3}$.</Q>
<Answer>$N = 0.500\ \text{mol} \times 6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \text{mol}^{-1} =$ **3.01 × 10²³ molecules**</Answer>

<Q n={9}>Moles in 1.50 × 10²⁴ molecules of water.</Q>
<Answer>$n = \dfrac{1.50 \times 10^{24}}{6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \text{mol}^{-1}} =$ **2.49 mol**</Answer>

<Q n={10}>Mass of 3.01 × 10²³ sodium atoms.</Q>
<Answer>

$n = \dfrac{3.01 \times 10^{23}}{6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \text{mol}^{-1}} = 0.500\ \text{mol}$

$m = 0.500\ \text{mol} \times 22.99\ \text{g/mol} =$ **11.5 g**

</Answer>

## D. Counting atoms inside compounds

<Q n={11}>Moles of H atoms in 2.00 mol of glucose.</Q>
<Answer>Each $\ce{C6H12O6}$ contains 12 H atoms: 2.00 mol × 12 = **24.0 mol of H atoms**</Answer>

<Q n={12}>Oxygen atoms in 10.0 g of $\ce{CO2}$.</Q>
<Answer>

$M(\ce{CO2}) = 12.01 + 2(16.00) = 44.01\ \text{g/mol}$

$n(\ce{CO2}) = \dfrac{10.0}{44.01} = 0.2272\ \text{mol}$

O atoms $= 2 \times 0.2272\ \text{mol} \times 6.022 \times 10^{23}\ \text{mol}^{-1} =$ **2.74 × 10²³ atoms**

</Answer>

<Q n={13}>Mass of chlorine in 1.00 mol of $\ce{MgCl2}$.</Q>
<Answer>1.00 mol of $\ce{MgCl2}$ contains 2.00 mol of Cl: 2.00 mol × 35.45 g/mol = **70.90 g**</Answer>

## E. Challenge

<Q n={14}>More molecules: 10.0 g of $\ce{H2O}$ or 10.0 g of $\ce{CO2}$?</Q>
<Answer>

$n(\ce{H2O}) = \dfrac{10.0}{18.02} = 0.555\ \text{mol}$ &nbsp;&nbsp; $n(\ce{CO2}) = \dfrac{10.0}{44.01} = 0.227\ \text{mol}$

**Water** contains more molecules (3.34 × 10²³ compared with 1.37 × 10²³). Water molecules are lighter, so the same mass contains more of them.

</Answer>

<Q n={15}>Identify the element.</Q>
<Answer>$M = \dfrac{m}{n} = \dfrac{5.00\ \text{g}}{0.125\ \text{mol}} = 40.0\ \text{g/mol}$. This matches **calcium** (Ca = 40.08).</Answer>
