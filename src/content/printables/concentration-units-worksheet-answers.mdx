## A. Ideas

<Q n={1}>Molarity and molality.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **Molarity** is moles of solute per **litre of solution**; **molality** is moles of solute per **kilogram of solvent**. (b) **Molarity** changes with temperature, because the volume of a solution expands or contracts. Molality, mass percent, ppm and mole fraction use **masses** and amounts, which do not change.</Answer>

## B. Mass-based units

<Q n={2}>Sugar.</Q>
<Answer>Mass of solution $= 12.0\ \text{g} + 188\ \text{g} = 200.\ \text{g}$; $\dfrac{12.0\ \text{g}}{200.\ \text{g}} \times 100\% = \textbf{6.00\%}$</Answer>

<Q n={3}>Fluoride.</Q>
<Answer>0.45 mg = 0.00045 g; $\dfrac{0.00045\ \text{g}}{500.\ \text{g}} \times 10^{6} = \textbf{0.90 ppm}$</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Saline.</Q>
<Answer>$0.0090 \times 500.\ \text{g} = \textbf{4.5 g NaCl}$</Answer>

## C. Moles-based units

<Q n={5}>Glucose.</Q>
<Answer>

Moles: glucose $\dfrac{18.0\ \text{g}}{180.16\ \text{g/mol}} = 0.09991\ \text{mol}$; water $\dfrac{90.0\ \text{g}}{18.02\ \text{g/mol}} = 4.994\ \text{mol}$

(a) $x = \dfrac{0.09991\ \text{mol}}{0.09991\ \text{mol} + 4.994\ \text{mol}} = \textbf{0.0196}$

(b) $m = \dfrac{0.09991\ \text{mol}}{0.0900\ \text{kg}} = \textbf{1.11 mol/kg}$

</Answer>

## D. Converting between units

<Q n={6}>Sulfuric acid.</Q>
<Answer>

Take 1.000 L: mass $= 1000\ \text{mL} \times 1.84\ \text{g/mL} = 1840\ \text{g}$

Mass of H₂SO₄ $= 0.980 \times 1840\ \text{g} = 1803\ \text{g}$; moles $= \dfrac{1803\ \text{g}}{98.08\ \text{g/mol}} = 18.38\ \text{mol}$

$c = \dfrac{18.38\ \text{mol}}{1.000\ \text{L}} = \textbf{18.4 mol/L}$

</Answer>

<Q n={7}>From molality to mass percent.</Q>
<Answer>

Take 1.000 kg of water: it contains 1.50 mol NaCl $= 1.50\ \text{mol} \times 58.44\ \text{g/mol} = 87.66\ \text{g}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\%\ \text{NaCl} = \frac{87.66\ \text{g}}{1000\ \text{g} + 87.66\ \text{g}} \times 100\% \\[4pt]
&= \textbf{8.06\%}
\end{aligned}
$$

</Answer>

## E. Challenge

<Q n={8}>From molality to molarity.</Q>
<Answer>

Take 1.000 kg of water: 3.00 mol NaCl $= 3.00\ \text{mol} \times 58.44\ \text{g/mol} = 175.3\ \text{g}$.

Mass of solution $= 1000\ \text{g} + 175.3\ \text{g} = 1175.3\ \text{g}$

Volume of solution $= \dfrac{1175.3\ \text{g}}{1.12\ \text{g/mL}} = 1049\ \text{mL} = 1.049\ \text{L}$

$c = \dfrac{3.00\ \text{mol}}{1.049\ \text{L}} = \textbf{2.86 mol/L}$

</Answer>
