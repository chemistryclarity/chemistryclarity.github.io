## A. IR spectroscopy

<Q n={1}>IR basics.</Q>
<Answer>(a) The bond **vibrates** more strongly (stretches or bends): it absorbs the energy of IR radiation at a wavenumber characteristic of that bond. (b) The region **below about 1500 cm⁻¹**: a complex pattern unique to each compound, **compared with a database** of known spectra to confirm an identity.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Functional groups.</Q>
<Answer>(a) **alcohol** (O–H and C–O) (b) **C=O** with no O–H: a ketone, aldehyde or ester (c) **carboxylic acid** (very broad O–H and C=O) (d) **nitrile**, C≡N</Answer>

<Q n={3}>Wavelengths.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) $\lambda = \dfrac{1}{3300\ \text{cm}^{-1}} = 3.030 \times 10^{-4}\ \text{cm} \times \dfrac{10^{4}\ \mu\text{m}}{1\ \text{cm}} = \textbf{3.03}\ \boldsymbol{\mu}\textbf{m}$

(b) $\lambda = \dfrac{1}{2250\ \text{cm}^{-1}} = 4.444 \times 10^{-4}\ \text{cm} = \textbf{4.44}\ \boldsymbol{\mu}\textbf{m}$

</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Three C₃ compounds.</Q>
<Answer>**Propan-1-ol**: broad O–H at 3200–3550 cm⁻¹, **no C=O**. **Propanal**: strong C=O at about 1730 cm⁻¹, **no O–H**. **Propanoic acid**: **both** a very broad O–H (2500–3300 cm⁻¹) and a C=O.</Answer>

## C. Challenge

<Q n={8}>The unknown.</Q>
<Answer>**Ethanoic acid**, $\ce{CH3COOH}$. Very broad 2500–3300 cm⁻¹: **acid O–H**; 1710 cm⁻¹: **C=O**. MS: **60** = molecular ion (C₂H₄O₂, 60 g/mol); **45** = $\ce{COOH+}$ (loss of CH₃); **43** = $\ce{CH3CO+}$ (loss of OH, 60 − 17); **15** = $\ce{CH3+}$.</Answer>
