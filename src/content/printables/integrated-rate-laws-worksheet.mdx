## B. Calculations

<Q n={3} box={4}>A drug is removed from the body by first-order kinetics with a half-life of 4.00 h. A patient takes 200. mg. (a) Find k. (b) How much remains after 10.0 h?</Q>

<Q n={4} box={3}>A first-order reaction has k = 0.0200 min⁻¹. How long does it take for [A] to fall from 0.800 M to 0.200 M?</Q>

<Q n={5} box={4}>A second-order reaction has k = 0.150 M⁻¹ s⁻¹ and [A]₀ = 0.0500 M. Find (a) the first half-life (b) [A] after 200. s.</Q>

<Q n={6} box={3}>A zero-order decomposition on a metal surface has k = 2.50 × 10⁻³ M/s and [A]₀ = 0.500 M. Find (a) the half-life (b) the time for all of A to be used up.</Q>

## C. Order from data

## D. Mechanisms

<Q n={8} lines={5}>A proposed mechanism is: step 1 $\ce{NO2Cl -> NO2 + Cl}$ (slow); step 2 $\ce{NO2Cl + Cl -> NO2 + Cl2}$ (fast). (a) Write the overall equation. (b) Identify the intermediate. (c) Write the rate law predicted by this mechanism.</Q>
