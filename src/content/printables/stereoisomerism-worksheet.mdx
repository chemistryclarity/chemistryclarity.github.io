## A. Types of isomer

## B. Cis–trans and E/Z

<Q n={2} box={3}>Which of these show cis–trans isomerism? Explain each. (a) pent-2-ene, $\ce{CH3CH=CHCH2CH3}$ (b) 2-methylpropene, $\ce{(CH3)2C=CH2}$ (c) 1,2-dibromoethene, $\ce{BrCH=CHBr}$</Q>

<Q n={3} box={3}>Draw the cis and trans isomers of 1,2-dichloroethene. Which has the higher boiling point (60 °C or 48 °C), and why?</Q>
