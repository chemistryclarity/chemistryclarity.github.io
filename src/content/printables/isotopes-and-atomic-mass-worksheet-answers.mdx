## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>What the atomic mass represents.</Q>
<Answer>The **weighted average** mass of the element's naturally occurring isotopes, based on their abundances.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Which isotope is more abundant?</Q>
<Answer>The **20.0 u** isotope: the average (20.2 u) is much closer to 20.0 than to 22.0, so the lighter isotope must be far more common.</Answer>
