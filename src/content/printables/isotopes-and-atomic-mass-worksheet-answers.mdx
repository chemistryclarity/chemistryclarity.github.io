## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>What the atomic mass represents.</Q>
<Answer>The **weighted average** mass of the element's naturally occurring isotopes, based on their abundances.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>Which isotope is more abundant?</Q>
<Answer>The **20.0 u** isotope: the average (20.2 u) is much closer to 20.0 u than to 22.0 u, so the lighter isotope must be far more common.</Answer>

## B. Calculating average atomic mass

Each abundance is written as a fraction (percentage ÷ 100), which has no unit.

<Q n={3}>Boron.</Q>
<Answer>(0.199 × 10.013 u) + (0.801 × 11.009 u) = **10.81 u**</Answer>

<Q n={4}>Rubidium.</Q>
<Answer>(0.7217 × 84.912 u) + (0.2783 × 86.909 u) = **85.47 u**</Answer>

<Q n={5}>Silver.</Q>
<Answer>(0.5184 × 106.905 u) + (0.4816 × 108.905 u) = **107.87 u**</Answer>

<Q n={6}>Silicon.</Q>
<Answer>(0.9223 × 27.977 u) + (0.0468 × 28.976 u) + (0.0309 × 29.974 u) = **28.09 u**</Answer>

## C. Challenge

<Q n={7}>Abundances of copper isotopes.</Q>
<Answer>

Let $x$ = fraction of copper-63 (no unit): $(62.930\ \text{u})x + (64.928\ \text{u})(1 - x) = 63.546\ \text{u}$

$64.928\ \text{u} - (1.998\ \text{u})x = 63.546\ \text{u}$, so $x = \dfrac{1.382\ \text{u}}{1.998\ \text{u}} = 0.692$

**Copper-63: 69.2%**, **copper-65: 30.8%**

</Answer>
