## A. Understanding

<Q n={1}>Hess's law.</Q>
<Answer>The enthalpy change of a reaction is **the same whatever route** is taken from reactants to products. It is true because enthalpy is a **state function** (it depends only on the initial and final states), which follows from the conservation of energy.</Answer>

<Q n={2}>ΔHf° = 0.</Q>
<Answer>**O₂(g), N₂(g), Fe(s), C(graphite)**: elements in their standard states. O₃(g) is not the standard state of oxygen; H₂O(l) and CO₂(g) are compounds.</Answer>

## B. Combining equations

<Q n={3}>Nitrogen dioxide.</Q>
<Answer>(a) The equations add directly; 2NO cancels: ΔH = +182.6 kJ + (−116.2 kJ) = **+66.4 kJ**. (b) This equation forms 2 mol of NO₂ from its elements, so ΔHf°(NO₂) = +66.4 kJ ÷ 2 mol = **+33.2 kJ/mol**.</Answer>

## C. Using enthalpies of formation

<Q n={4}>Three reactions.</Q>
<Answer>

(a) ΔH° = (1 mol)(−84.0 kJ/mol) − [(1 mol)(+52.4 kJ/mol) + 0 kJ] = **−136.4 kJ**

(b) ΔH° = [(1 mol)(−634.9 kJ/mol) + (1 mol)(−393.5 kJ/mol)] − (1 mol)(−1207.6 kJ/mol) = −1028.4 kJ + 1207.6 kJ = **+179.2 kJ** (endothermic: limestone must be heated strongly)

(c) ΔH° = [(1 mol)(−1675.7 kJ/mol) + 0 kJ] − [(1 mol)(−824.2 kJ/mol) + 0 kJ] = **−851.5 kJ** (very exothermic: it melts the iron)

</Answer>

<Q n={5}>Propane.</Q>
<Answer>

Products: (3 mol)(−393.5 kJ/mol) + (4 mol)(−285.8 kJ/mol) = −1180.5 kJ − 1143.2 kJ = −2323.7 kJ

Reactants: (1 mol)(−103.8 kJ/mol) + (5 mol)(0 kJ/mol) = −103.8 kJ

ΔH° = −2323.7 kJ − (−103.8 kJ) = **−2219.9 kJ**

</Answer>

<Q n={6}>Ammonia oxidation.</Q>
<Answer>

Products: (4 mol)(+91.3 kJ/mol) + (6 mol)(−241.8 kJ/mol) = 365.2 kJ − 1450.8 kJ = −1085.6 kJ

Reactants: (4 mol)(−45.9 kJ/mol) + (5 mol)(0 kJ/mol) = −183.6 kJ

ΔH° = −1085.6 kJ − (−183.6 kJ) = **−902.0 kJ**

</Answer>

<Q n={7}>Vaporization of water.</Q>
<Answer>ΔH° = (1 mol)(−241.8 kJ/mol) − (1 mol)(−285.8 kJ/mol) = **+44.0 kJ**. This is the **enthalpy of vaporization** of water at 25 °C: the heat needed to turn 1 mol of liquid water into vapour.</Answer>

## D. Challenge

<Q n={8}>ΔHf° of ethanol.</Q>
<Answer>

ΔH° = [2ΔHf°(CO₂) + 3ΔHf°(H₂O(l))] − [ΔHf°(ethanol) + 0], so

ΔHf°(ethanol) × (1 mol) = (2 mol)(−393.5 kJ/mol) + (3 mol)(−285.8 kJ/mol) − (−1366.8 kJ)

= −787.0 kJ − 857.4 kJ + 1366.8 kJ = −277.6 kJ, so ΔHf°(ethanol) = **−277.6 kJ/mol** (matching the table)

</Answer>

<Q n={9}>Glucose.</Q>
<Answer>

Products: (6 mol)(−393.5 kJ/mol) + (6 mol)(−285.8 kJ/mol) = −2361.0 kJ − 1714.8 kJ = −4075.8 kJ

ΔH° = −4075.8 kJ − (1 mol)(−1273.3 kJ/mol) = **−2802.5 kJ** (per mole of glucose)

Energy from 10.0 g: $\dfrac{10.0\ \text{g}}{180.16\ \text{g/mol}} = 0.05551\ \text{mol}$; 0.05551 mol × 2802.5 kJ/mol = **156 kJ** released

</Answer>
