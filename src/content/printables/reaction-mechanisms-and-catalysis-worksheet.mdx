## A. Elementary steps

<Q n={1} box={3}>Give the molecularity and the rate law of each elementary step: (a) $\ce{O3 -> O2 + O}$ (b) $\ce{NO + O3 -> NO2 + O2}$ (c) $\ce{2NO2 -> N2O4}$</Q>

<Q n={2} box={4}>Hydrogen peroxide decomposes in the presence of iodide ions: step 1 (slow) $\ce{H2O2 + I- -> H2O + IO-}$; step 2 (fast) $\ce{H2O2 + IO- -> H2O + O2 + I-}$. (a) Write the overall equation. (b) Identify the catalyst and the intermediate. (c) Write the rate law. (d) Is this homogeneous or heterogeneous catalysis?</Q>

## B. Testing mechanisms

<Q n={3} box={4}>For $\ce{2NO + O2 -> 2NO2}$, rate $= k[\ce{NO}]^2[\ce{O2}]$. Mechanism A is one termolecular step. Mechanism B is $\ce{2NO <=> N2O2}$ (fast), then $\ce{N2O2 + O2 -> 2NO2}$ (slow). (a) Show that both mechanisms give the experimental rate law. (b) Which is more likely, and why?</Q>

<Q n={4} box={4}>Ozone decomposes, $\ce{2O3 -> 3O2}$, by: $\ce{O3 <=> O2 + O}$ (fast), then $\ce{O + O3 -> 2O2}$ (slow). Derive the rate law, and explain why adding $\ce{O2}$ slows the reaction.</Q>

## C. Catalysts and enzymes

<Q n={6} box={4}>Using the enzyme data, calculate the rate at (a) $[\ce{S}] = 0.50$ mmol/L (b) $[\ce{S}] = 4.5$ mmol/L. (c) At what [S] is the rate 90 % of $V_\text{max}$? (d) At $[\ce{S}] = 0.050$ mmol/L, compare the exact rate with the low-[S] approximation $v \approx (V_\text{max}/K_\text{m})[\ce{S}]$.</Q>

## D. Challenge

Inhibitor Y is non-competitive ($V_\text{max}$ falls to 90 µmol L⁻¹ min⁻¹, $K_\text{m}$ unchanged). (a) Calculate the rate with no inhibitor, with X and with Y. (b) Explain how measuring the rates at $[\ce{S}] = 10$ mmol/L would tell the two inhibitors apart.</Q>

<Q n={8} box={3}>The enzyme concentration in the experiment is 0.025 µmol/L. (a) Calculate the turnover number in $\text{s}^{-1}$. (b) Explain why the rate of the enzyme reaction first rises and then falls as the temperature is raised from 20 °C to 70 °C.</Q>
