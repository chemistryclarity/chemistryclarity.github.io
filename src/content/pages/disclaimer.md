Chemistry Clarity provides general educational content about chemistry. We work hard to keep it accurate, but we cannot guarantee that every page is complete, current or free of errors.

- **Not a substitute for your course.** Always follow the syllabus, materials and instructions given by your teacher, institution or exam board.
- **Laboratory safety.** Nothing on this site is an instruction to carry out experiments. Practical work should only be done under appropriate supervision, with proper safety equipment and procedures.
- **Simplified explanations.** Some lessons deliberately simplify ideas for learning purposes. Where it matters, we note the more precise version.

If you find an error, please [let us know](../contact/).
