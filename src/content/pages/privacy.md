This page explains what information is collected when you use Chemistry Clarity.

## Browsing the website

Chemistry Clarity **does not** use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and has no user accounts. You can read every lesson without giving us any personal information.

## Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

## Newsletter

If you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your **email address** and use it **only** to send you the Chemistry Clarity newsletter (new lessons, study tips, free resources and occasional news about Chemistry Clarity resources).

- Your address is sent directly to, and stored by, our email service provider, **MailerLite**, which sends the newsletter on our behalf. See MailerLite's privacy policy for how it handles data.
- MailerLite may record whether newsletter emails are opened or links are clicked, which helps us understand which content is useful.
- We **never sell** your email address.
- You can **unsubscribe at any time** using the link at the bottom of every newsletter. You can also email us to ask us to delete your address.

### Age

The newsletter sign-up asks you to confirm that you are 16 or older, or that you have permission from a parent or guardian.

## Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you.

## Changes

If we add features that collect information (for example analytics), we will update this page before they go live.
