This page explains what information is collected when you visit Chemistry Clarity.

## What we collect

At present, Chemistry Clarity **does not** use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, user accounts or sign-up forms, and we do not collect personal information through the website.

## Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

## Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you.

## Changes

If we add features that collect information (for example, analytics or a newsletter), we will update this page before they go live.
